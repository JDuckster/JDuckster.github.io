SuperMaxDuck

A private, single-user automation project.

SuperMaxDuck is a set of small automation scripts that I run on my own computer for my own use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What it does

The first agent, Morning Briefing, runs once each morning and:

  • reads my upcoming Google Calendar events,
  • reviews recent messages in my Gmail inbox to find ones that may need attention,
  • lists Google Drive files changed in the last day (names and dates only), and
  • sends a single summary email to my own inbox.

It accesses only the Google account of its owner, who signed in and granted access.
